      Put the guard in your field · खेतमा पहरा राख्ने तरिका

      An old Android phone becomes a watchman: its camera sees the monkeys, the app decides, and a loudspeaker scares them. Everything runs on the phone, no internet needed after the first load.

      Set up the phone

      1. Open KhetiSathi on the phone in Chrome. The live camera needs a secure web address (https) or the KhetiSathi file opened directly in Chrome on a laptop. Put the file on a free host such as Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages, then open that link on the phone.
      2. Add it to the home screen from the Chrome menu so it opens like an app.
      3. Choose Live camera and allow camera access. Point it at the path monkeys use from the forest.
      4. Mark ignore areas over trees and roads that move or carry people.
      5. Connect a loud speaker by Bluetooth or cable. The phone speaker alone is too quiet for a field.
      6. Set guard hours, keep the sounds varied, then press Arm guard. The screen stays on while the guard is armed.

      Mount it on a pole

      1. Use a 2–3 m bamboo pole at the field corner facing the forest edge.
      2. Put the phone in a clear plastic box or a cut bottle so rain cannot reach it. Keep the lens clean.
      3. Tilt the camera down so the sky is mostly out of the picture. Moving clouds cause false alarms.
      4. Place the speaker facing the forest, not your house.
      5. Power it with a 20 W solar panel and a power bank or a small 12 V battery with charge controller.
      Old Android phoneAndroid 8 or newer, working back camera
      Loudspeaker10 W or more, Bluetooth or AUX
      Power20,000 mAh power bank, or 20 W solar panel + battery
      HousingPlastic box, bamboo pole, cable ties
      OptionalLED flood light for the torch-style flash at dusk

      Stop monkeys getting used to it

      • Monkeys learn fast. One repeated sound stops working in days. Keep at least four sounds on so the app mixes them.
      • Move the pole every week or two, and use Patrol mode as well.
      • Now and then, follow an alarm by walking out with a dog or a stick. Monkeys learn the sound means a person is coming.
      • Clear bushes 10–20 m from the field edge so monkeys have no cover to wait in.
      • Never leave food waste or fallen fruit near the field.
      • Guard in turns with neighbours. A whole slope guarded together works far better than one field.
      • Scare, never hurt. Monkeys and langurs are respected and can bite when cornered.

      Use the Visits by hour chart to see when raids happen, then plan your own rounds for those hours.

      No phone? Build a sensor post · सेन्सर पोल

      An Arduino Nano with a motion sensor plays random scare sounds from an SD card and flashes a light. Cost is low, and it runs for days on a 12 V battery.
